Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Neon Pixel Studios ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the mobile game Flappy Verse.

1. Information We Collect

Flappy Verse does not require users to create an account or provide personal information directly to us to play the game.

Game progress, scores, coins, and preferences may be stored locally on your device. This information is used to support gameplay.

Our advertising provider may automatically collect certain information, such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, and app activity, as described in Google's privacy documentation.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

Flappy Verse uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may use device information, advertising identifiers, and other information to provide, measure, and improve advertisements, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.

Rewarded advertisements may offer in-game rewards, such as coins or an opportunity to revive a character. Rewards are granted according to the game's advertising reward mechanism.

For more information about Google's data practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy.

3. How Information Is Used

Information handled through the game and its advertising services may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain game functionality.
  • Save game progress and preferences on your device.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Prevent fraud and maintain advertising security.
  • Improve the game's performance and user experience.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information collected directly by us. Information may be processed by Google AdMob and its service providers as necessary to provide advertising and related services. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Game progress and related gameplay information may remain stored locally on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. Information processed by third-party advertising providers is retained according to their applicable policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Flappy Verse is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

Advertising and age-related settings are subject to the applicable Google Play and Google AdMob requirements.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal information processed by advertising providers. You can manage available advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and Google's privacy controls.

8. Third-Party Services

The game uses third-party services, including Google AdMob. These services may process information under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
